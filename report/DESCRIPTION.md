# Riaya App Overview

Riaya is an **AI-powered doctor appointment platform based in Tunisia**, supporting multiple doctors and allowing patients to book appointments through an AI voice assistant.

**Patients have no web portal.** Their interactions with Riaya are handled entirely through **phone calls and WhatsApp**.

### Technology Stack

- **Next.js:** Frontend and backend. **TailwindCSS** for styling.
- **Express.js:** WebSocket management and WhatsApp integration via **WhatsApp Baileys**.
- **PostgreSQL:** Main application database, accessed through **Drizzle ORM**.
- **OpenAI Realtime API + Microsoft Azure:** AI voice agent.
- **Twilio:** Receiving and handling patient phone calls.
- **Cloudflare R2:** Storage for uploaded medical documents and files.
- **BullMQ + Redis:** Background jobs and scheduled tasks.
- **Docker + GitHub Actions + Ubuntu VPS:** Containerized infra and automated production deploys.



### Authentication

Doctors authenticate using **Better Auth**. When a doctor tries to create an account, Riaya sends them a one-time password (**OTP**) by email using **Nodemailer**. The OTP is stored in the **Drizzle verification table**. The doctor must enter the OTP to complete registration, after which the account is created.

### Doctor Registration

Doctors can create accounts and provide their **first name, last name, CIN picture, speciality, Medical Council registration number, Medical Council certificate, cabinet name, TIN, cabinet city, and exact cabinet location selected from a map**.

After registration, doctors can only access their **profile page** where they complete and submit this information.

Their account must then be **reviewed by an administrator**:

- **Approved** — the doctor gains access to the full dashboard.
- **Rejected** — the administrator can attach rejection reasons. The doctor stays limited to the profile page, can correct their information, and **reapply**.
- Until the account is verified, all other pages and features of the dashboard remain locked.

### Doctor Dashboard

After their account is approved, doctors can access the full dashboard to manage their daily activities.

- **Home**
  - The home page shows an overview of the practice, with **stats and visualizations** of appointments, patients, and usage.
- **Patients**
  - Doctors can manage their patients and create new patients manually or through appointments generated by the AI.
- **Appointments**
  - Doctors can create and manage appointments through the calendar. New appointments are displayed in real time using WebSockets.
- **Availability**
  - Doctors set their weekly working hours. The AI uses this availability when searching for appointment slots.
- **Consultations**
  - Doctors can manage consultation notes, prescriptions, and medical documents related to their patients.
- **Invoices**
  - Doctors can create and manage invoices for their patients and send them directly through WhatsApp.
- **Subscriptions**
  - Doctors can subscribe to a plan, track their usage, and check their invoices from a dedicated subscriptions page.
- **Conversation recordings**
  - While talking with a patient, doctors can record the conversation and later generate a **transcript** so that everything is documented.
- **AI assistant**
  - Doctors have a chatbot they can ask about anything in the app, including what they can and cannot do and how to do things. They can also ask **medical questions**, and they can assign a generated transcript from a recording so they can ask questions about it.
- **WhatsApp configuration**
  - Doctors have a WhatsApp config page where they scan a **QR code** to link their WhatsApp phone. All messages are then sent from that number.



### Subscriptions

Riaya offers two subscription plans: a **Free** tier and a **Pro** tier.

- **Free**
  - Doctors have access to the main dashboard features, including **patient and appointment management, invoices, and medical files**.
  - Advanced features are limited: **5 AI phone bookings per month**, **50 WhatsApp messages per month**, **5 conversation recordings per month**, and **20 AI messages per month**.
- **Pro**
  - Unlimited access to all features.

Payments are processed through the **Konnect.network API**. Recurring invoices are handled by a **daily BullMQ job** that checks for subscriptions ending soon and generates invoices for them.

Doctors can view these invoices in their dashboard and **must pay them in order to keep their subscription active**.

### WhatsApp Integration

WhatsApp is managed using **WhatsApp Baileys**.

Doctors have a **WhatsApp configuration page** where they scan a **QR code** to link their own WhatsApp phone. Once connected, all messages are sent from the doctor's own phone number.

Because messages go out through the doctor's own number, the conversation between the doctor and the patient **stays private**.

Once their WhatsApp account is connected, doctors can:

- Send medical documents to their patients directly through WhatsApp.
- Send invoices to patients through WhatsApp.
- Send appointment confirmations to patients.
- Perform these actions directly from the dashboard with a single click.



### AI Doctor Appointment System

When a patient needs to see a doctor, they can call the **Riaya phone number**. Calls are received through **Twilio** and connected to the AI voice assistant.

When the patient dials, Twilio sends a webhook to the realtime **Express** server. The server looks up or creates the caller, then answers with **TwiML** that opens a **WebSocket media stream** from Twilio to that server.

Each call is a session that bridges three connections in real time:

- **Twilio WebSocket** — live audio from the patient's phone.
- **OpenAI Realtime WebSocket** (through **Microsoft Azure**) — speech-to-speech with the AI agent, including tool calls back into Riaya.
- **Dashboard WebSocket** — live transcript so administrators can monitor the call.

The patient's speech is streamed to the AI, and the AI's voice is streamed back through Twilio onto the phone. When the call ends, the Twilio stream closes and the session is marked completed.

An AI voice assistant built using the **OpenAI Realtime API through Microsoft Azure** answers the call and communicates with the patient in real time. The AI agent can communicate in **English, French, and Tunisian Arabic**. It does **not** give medical advice.

Each patient has a **preferred language**. It **defaults to Arabic**, and can be updated to **English** or **French** if the patient asks the AI to speak in that language. WhatsApp messages are then sent to the patient in that language, and the AI will speak in that language the next time the patient calls.

The patient's **phone number is used as their unique identifier**. The number is taken from the incoming call, so the AI never asks for it. When a patient calls, Riaya checks whether a person already exists with that phone number. If they do, it fetches their profile (**first name, last name, date of birth, gender, address, and preferred language**) and their **existing AI appointments**. The AI uses the patient's name when greeting them.

For existing bookings, Riaya loads **upcoming pending and confirmed appointments** (doctor name, cabinet name, address, date and time, status, and the reason for the visit), plus a **recent past appointment** if there is one. The patient can ask about a booking, the doctor, the location, or the time. They can **cancel a pending appointment** over the phone. If the appointment is already **confirmed**, the AI cannot cancel it and tells the patient to contact the doctor's office.

#### Booking flow

If the patient wants a new appointment, the AI follows this order, asking **one question at a time**:

1. **Greet** the caller (by name if it is already on file). If the name is unknown, ask for it and save it.
2. Ask for the **reason for the visit or symptoms**, unless the patient already names a speciality or type of doctor. Map that to a medical speciality, tell the patient the speciality name in their language, and ask them to confirm it.
3. Ask for their **location** (city and a more specific area such as a region or street).
4. Ask for their **preferred date and time** (treated as Tunisia local time, GMT+1).
5. Run the **best-fit search** (described below). The AI presents the **top 2–3 options**: doctor name, cabinet, address, approximate distance, and time slot.
6. The patient can pick an option, ask for **other times**, or ask for slots with a **specific doctor**. The AI keeps searching until they choose a slot or decide to stop.
7. When they confirm a slot, the AI **books a pending appointment** with the chosen doctor, the patient's name, the reason for the visit, and the selected time. If booking fails (slot taken, doctor unavailable, or the doctor's **Free-plan AI booking limit**), the AI tells the patient and offers another slot.
8. The AI confirms the booking in one sentence and ends the call.



#### Emergency mode

If the AI senses that the situation is **urgent**, it confirms with the patient that they want the **closest available doctors right away**. When the patient agrees:

1. Riaya runs an **ASAP best-fit search** (preferred time = now, same 50 km radius rules).
2. It creates **up to 3 pending appointments** for the top matching doctors, all marked **urgent** and linked by a shared **emergency group id**.
3. Doctors see an **urgent badge** on those requests in the calendar and get a stronger live notification.
4. The **first doctor to accept** keeps the appointment. The remaining pending requests in that group are **cancelled automatically**.
5. If the patient later cancels one of those pending urgent requests by phone, **the whole group is cancelled**.

A doctor who only declines their own urgent request does not cancel the siblings — other doctors can still accept.

**Plan limits do not apply** to emergency fan-out: Free-tier doctors who have already used their monthly AI booking quota are still included, so urgent patients are not blocked by subscription caps.

#### Best-fit matching

To place the patient with the right doctor, Riaya first resolves their location, then ranks doctors who can actually see them.

**Location search.** The AI asks for the city and a more specific area (region or street) separately, then geocodes that pair with **Geoapify**. The search is **restricted to Tunisia**, limited to a few results, and if several matches come back the AI asks the patient to pick one. If a precise place cannot be found, it falls back to a **known list of Tunisian cities** with stored GPS coordinates. Doctor cabinets are stored with **exact latitude and longitude** from the registration map, so matching is GPS-to-GPS rather than city-name matching. Distance is computed with the **Haversine** formula (kilometers between the patient and the cabinet).

**Radius filter.** Doctors further than **50 km** from the patient are excluded before any scoring. This prevents cross-city matches — for example, a Sfax doctor being offered to a Tunis patient just because that speciality has no closer provider.

**Slot search.** Only **verified** doctors with the chosen speciality and a mapped cabinet remain. For each, Riaya finds the nearest free **30-minute slot** around the preferred time within the next week, skipping times that overlap existing appointments. It searches both before and after the requested time and keeps the slot closest to what the patient asked for. The response also includes **2–3 additional forward slots** per doctor so the AI can offer alternatives within the same response without a second search.

**Scoring.** Each candidate is scored as **distance (km) + |slot − preferred time| (minutes) / 10**. The time delta is measured against what the patient actually asked for (not against the current moment), so a doctor 3 km away on Thursday at 10:00 always beats a doctor 40 km away who happens to be free this afternoon. The list is sorted by that score (lower is better).

Administrators can run the same search from a **best-fit map and calendar** to inspect which doctors match a speciality, location, and date range.

#### After the booking

- **Pending appointments**
  - AI-created appointments initially appear as pending on the selected doctor's calendar in real time. The doctor can review the patient details, symptoms, and timing, then **accept or decline**.
- **Accept**
  - The appointment becomes confirmed. If no patient record is linked yet, the doctor can match the caller by phone, create a new patient, or pick an existing one. Riaya then sends a **WhatsApp confirmation** to the patient in their preferred language.
- **Decline**
  - The appointment is cancelled.
- **Unanswered pending timeout**
  - If a pending AI request (or an urgent group with no accept) stays unanswered for **10 minutes**, Riaya **auto-cancels** it.
  - The platform then tries to **call the patient back** with a short Twilio message asking them to call Riaya again to book with another doctor.
  - If outbound calling is not configured or fails, Riaya sends a **WhatsApp** message (via the admin WhatsApp account) with the same ask.
- **Appointment cancellation by the patient**
  - Patients can call Riaya again to cancel as long as the appointment is still **pending**. Confirmed appointments can only be cancelled by contacting the doctor's office.
- **Post-appointment review**
  - When the doctor accepts, a WhatsApp review message is scheduled with **BullMQ** for **two hours after the appointment ends**.
  - The message contains a link where the patient can **rate the doctor directly without creating an account**.



### Admin Dashboard

Administrators have a dedicated dashboard for managing doctors and monitoring the AI appointment system.

- **Home**
  - The home page shows an overview of the platform, with **stats and visualizations** of doctors, appointments, and calls.
- **Doctor visualization and search**
  - Administrators can browse and search registered doctors, and run the **best-fit** search on a map and calendar to see which doctors match a speciality, location, and time.
- **Doctor verification**
  - Administrators can review newly registered doctors and **approve** or **reject** their applications (with rejection reasons). Approval unlocks the rest of the platform; rejection lets the doctor fix their profile and reapply.
- **Live call monitoring**
  - Administrators can monitor ongoing calls between the AI agent and patients in real time, including the live conversation transcript.

### Deployment

Riaya is split into **two repositories**: the **web** app (Next.js) and the **voice** realtime server (Express / Twilio / WhatsApp / workers). Each repo has its own **GitHub Actions** CI/CD workflow.

On push to **main**, GitHub-hosted runners **build Docker images**, publish them to **GitHub Container Registry (GHCR)**, and deploy the updated containers to a self-hosted **Ubuntu VPS**.

On the VPS:

- **Nginx** sits in front of the app containers as a reverse proxy (TLS / routing).
- **PostgreSQL** and **Redis** run as Docker containers on a shared Docker network.
- The web and voice app containers run via **Docker Compose** and connect to that infra.
- Web deploys run **Drizzle migrations** before the new container is brought up.
